FIELD NOTES

Four things worth knowing

01

Price is a path

Each tick becomes part of the chart. A sequence can look meaningful after the fact without being reliably predictable beforehand.

PRICE ACTION
02

Volatility changes

Quiet periods can give way to sudden jumps. A model that is calm one moment can become turbulent the next.

RISK AWARENESS
03

Direction has a cost

Long and short positions both carry risk. Position size can matter as much as your view of the next move.

POSITION SIZING
04

Manage risk

Define risk before entry and remember that historical patterns do not guarantee future outcomes.
